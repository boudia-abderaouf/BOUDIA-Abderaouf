Enhanced Foundation Model-Based Interactive Segmentation for Borehole Image Data

Imane Baho*, Eya Ghamgui*, Abderaouf Boudia, Franco Marchesoni, Josselin Kherroubi — third author; *the first two authors are marked in the paper as having contributed equally.

IEEE IGARSS 2025 · SLB, France (co-affiliations UVSQ, ENS-PS) · Published · DOI not recorded

SAM and its weakly-supervised adaptation WeSAM both underperform on ultrasonic borehole imagery, where a moulded pipe's manufacturing patterns mimic corrosion defects. The paper improves WeSAM in two places, both at the prompt end of the architecture: error-driven iterative click simulation in place of random point sampling, and SegNext-style dense prompt encoding that feeds the previous mask back into the next interaction. Evaluated on the THBK pipe-thickness dataset — 180 ultrasonic images with binary masks — the combined approach leads at every click budget, for a reported gain of over 13 IoU points.

Fall Risk Assessment Using Gait Analysis and Wearable Sensors: A Machine Learning Approach

W. Dib, A. Boudia, F. Boukhedimi, O. Kerdjidj — second author.

Submitted to an IEEE journal · Center for Development of Advanced Technologies (CDTA), Algiers & École Nationale Polytechnique, Algeria · Under review · no DOI — not yet published

Clinical fall-risk instruments are accurate and impractical; most sensor alternatives in the literature buy accuracy with many body-mounted sensors or with a network too heavy for a wearable. This study constrains itself to a single lower-back accelerometer and measures the accuracy-versus-cost trade-off explicitly. Four published gait-cycle segmentation algorithms are implemented and compared, per-cycle statistical and spatio-temporal features extracted, PCA applied at 99 % retained variance, and eight classifiers benchmarked on 71 older adults from the public PhysioNet LTMM database.

The finding is the trade-off, not the headline number: KNN and the deep network are level on accuracy and separated by more than three orders of magnitude in execution time.
