## Enhanced Foundation Model-Based Interactive Segmentation for Borehole Image Data

**Authors** — Imane Baho\*, Eya Ghamgui\*, **Abderaouf Boudia**, Franco Marchesoni,
Josselin Kherroubi
\*The first two authors are marked in the paper as having contributed equally.
My position in the author list is **third**.

**Affiliation** — SLB, France (co-affiliations: UVSQ, ENS-PS)
**Venue** — IEEE International Geoscience and Remote Sensing Symposium (IGARSS) 2025
**Status** — Published
**PDF** — [`Paper_IGARSS_InterSEG.pdf`](Paper_IGARSS_InterSEG.pdf)
**DOI** — not recorded here (add once the IEEE Xplore identifier is available)

**Summary** — SAM and its weakly-supervised adaptation WeSAM both underperform on
ultrasonic borehole imagery, where manufacturing patterns mimic corrosion defects. The
paper improves WeSAM with two changes — error-driven iterative click simulation instead
of random point prompts, and SegNext-style dense prompt encoding — reporting a gain of
over 13 IoU points on the THBK pipe-thickness dataset.

**Research area** — computer vision, interactive image segmentation, weakly-supervised
source-free domain adaptation, foundation models, non-destructive evaluation

## Fall Risk Assessment Using Gait Analysis and Wearable Sensors: A Machine Learning Approach

**Authors** — W. Dib, **A. Boudia**, F. Boukhedimi, O. Kerdjidj
My position in the author list is **second**.

**Affiliations** — Center for Development of Advanced Technologies (CDTA), Algiers;
École Nationale Polytechnique, Algeria
**Status** — Manuscript submitted to an IEEE journal — under review
**PDF** — [`Fall_Risk_Assessment_Using_Gait_Analysis.pdf`](Fall_Risk_Assessment_Using_Gait_Analysis.pdf)
**DOI** — none; not yet published

**Summary** — A single lower-back accelerometer, four gait-cycle segmentation algorithms
compared, per-cycle time/frequency/spatio-temporal features, PCA at 99 % retained
variance, and a benchmark of eight classifiers on 71 older adults from the public
PhysioNet LTMM database. KNN reaches 89.7 % test accuracy at 0.008 s inference —
statistically comparable to a deep network that takes 29 s.

**Research area** — biomedical signal processing, inertial sensing, gait analysis,
machine learning, wearable health
